We’re celebrating a milestone for **Justin Gambill: the submission of his first research paper**!

His work with Chase Mason and Jordan A. Dowell explores untargeted metabolomics of plant samples using HPLC with diode-array detection and Gaussian mixture models. The approach connects analytical chemistry with statistical classification to study variation in plant chemical profiles.

A preprint, **“Untargeted Metabolomics of Plant Samples using HPLC-DAD and Gaussian Mixture Models,”** is now available. This is a manuscript submission and preprint milestone; the work has not yet completed peer review.

Congratulations, Justin!

[Read the preprint](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.11.10.687686).
