The Dowell lab is at the **Plant Science Symposium** today, sharing research and connecting with the broader plant science community.

Our students have been preparing presentations and posters, and it’s a pleasure to see that work out in the world. Explaining a project to a new audience is an important part of doing science: it sharpens the questions, invites useful feedback, and opens up new conversations.

Congratulations to everyone presenting today! We’re celebrating the work that went into getting here—and taking a few group photos along the way.
