Our undergraduate researchers are sharing their work at **Discover Day** today, with lab members there to cheer them on and talk science.

A poster presentation brings together many parts of a research project: deciding on a question, carrying out the work, making sense of the results, and explaining why they matter. We’re proud to see our students taking ownership of that process and sharing it with the campus community.

Congratulations to everyone presenting, and thank you to the mentors and labmates supporting them. These photos capture a few of the conversations and celebrations from the day.
