Our latest field photos capture a different side of lab life: **working along marked rows, coordinating as a team, and making the most of the evening light**.

Fieldwork connects our questions about plant traits and interactions to the places where plants grow. It also takes a lot of practical teamwork, from preparing a site to keeping track of plants and helping one another through the day’s tasks.

Here’s a look at the people behind that work—and a few well-earned pauses for a group photo.
