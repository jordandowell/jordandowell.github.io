Jordan joined host **Shiran Ben Zeev** on the Botanical Society of America’s *A Botanical Podcast* to discuss how plants use chemistry to communicate and defend themselves. The conversation also explores life in challenging environments and the evolution of chemical diversity.

The episode, **“Plants Communicating Through Chemistry: A Conversation with Jordan Dowell,”** is Season 1, Episode 2, released May 19, 2025.
