Congratulations to Natalie Roberts on earning **first place in the STEM poster presentation category at Discover Day 2026**!

Natalie’s project, **“Directed Growth Responses of Botrytis cinerea to Plant-Derived Terpenes,”** explores a question at the center of our research: how does a fungal pathogen respond to the chemical environment created by its plant hosts?

The award recognizes Natalie’s work communicating this research through a scientific poster. We’re delighted to celebrate this achievement and the contributions of undergraduate researchers to the lab.
