The Dowell lab is preparing to move to new space in LSU’s Life Sciences Building!

The transition is an opportunity to think carefully about how we organize our shared work areas for plant biology, fungal research, and chemical analysis. As preparations get underway, the group is coordinating equipment, supplies, and workspaces to support our ongoing projects.

Thank you to everyone contributing to the move. Building a productive research environment takes teamwork, and we appreciate the effort going into this next stage for the lab.
