Members of the Dowell Research Group are in Palm Springs, California, for **Botany 2025**.

The meeting is an opportunity to connect with the wider plant science community and spend time together outside our everyday routines at LSU. Our research links plant chemistry, physiology, and evolution, and meetings like Botany provide a setting to exchange perspectives across those areas.

We’re glad to share the week with colleagues and fellow researchers.

[Meet the group](/people/) and [explore our plant traits and evolution theme](/research/plantphys2025/).

The presentation photos offer a closer look at the science we brought to the meeting: fungal morphology and pathogen traits, networks connecting plant nutritional and chemical traits with physiology, *Botrytis* biology, and experimental approaches to fungal physiology.
