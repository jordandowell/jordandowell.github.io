Congratulations to **Evelin Reyes**, who earned **third place in the lightning-talk presentation session at the 15th Annual Plant Sciences Symposium**!

Lightning talks challenge researchers to communicate their work clearly in a short amount of time. Evelin’s recognition celebrates her scientific communication and her participation in the plant science community.

We’re proud to celebrate this milestone with Evelin and look forward to seeing where her research takes her next.
