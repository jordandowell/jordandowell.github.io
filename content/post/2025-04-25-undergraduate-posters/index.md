It’s poster day for **Jenna and Kailey**, and the group is turning out to support their undergraduate research.

Their posters bring the work of the lab into a new setting: explaining questions, methods, and results to visitors who may be encountering these projects for the first time. The photos capture both sides of that experience—focused scientific conversations and the smiles that come with sharing work you’ve put time into.

Thank you to our student researchers and the mentors who helped them prepare. We’re rounding off the day by getting together for dinner to celebrate.
